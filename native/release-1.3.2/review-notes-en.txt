Version 1.3.2 changes only the first run: the sample chart is now a three-colour Fair Isle OXO band (original design), and the app opens straight into it and the guide (see the business-model paragraph below). No new features.

1.3.1 added one offline editing option: below the yarn palette, 「この色を変更」 opens the standard iOS colour picker for the selected yarn; every cell painted with that yarn takes the new colour (cells keep their yarn, so it is undoable with 「元に戻す」). The same works by tapping a yarn swatch in Settings (設定・保存) > 毛糸の色. It is an editing feature, so it follows the same lock and trial rules as the other editing tools.

1.3.0 added one offline feature: Settings (設定・保存) > 作品を保存・読み込み > 「編み図をPDFで書き出す（印刷用）」. The user picks paper (A4, B5, A3, Letter), orientation, colour / colour + symbols / black-and-white symbols, and cell size; page 1 is previewed. 「PDFを作成」 renders the chart pages on the device and opens the standard share sheet (Print, Save to Files, etc.). Nothing is uploaded and no network is used. It works while the chart is locked and after the trial, because it only reads the project (viewing and export stay available after the trial, as before).

Features from earlier versions, all fully on-device with no network, account or server:
- 1.2.2: 「文字から編み図をつくる」 has a 書体 (typeface) menu with six bundled fonts (Misaki, PixelMplus, Zen Maru Gothic, Dela Gothic One, Yusei Magic, Pacifico), all licensed for commercial use and redistribution (SIL OFL 1.1, M+ FONT LICENSE, Misaki licence); licences and credits are linked from the in-app help page (使い方). Stitch and row counts follow the entered text.
- 1.2.1: 「丸ヨークの編み図をつくる」 calculates a top-down yoke from gauge and size. A skippable three-step guide follows the first-launch trial sheet. The standard StoreKit review request (AppStore.requestReview) is called at most once, after 20 completed rows, never at launch or around purchase sheets.
- 1.2.0: 「写真から2〜3色の編み図をつくる」 converts a user-chosen photo into a 2- or 3-yarn chart locally; the photo never leaves the device.

Business model (unchanged since 1.1.0): free download + non-consumable 7-day trial (jp.amimononote.ios.trial7, JPY 0) + non-consumable permanent unlock (jp.amimononote.ios.lifetime, JPY 3000). No subscription, login or external payment. Since 1.3.2 the app opens straight into the sample chart and a skippable three-step guide; the trial terms sheet is shown when the user first tries to edit or count (and from the top banner). It states the 7-day duration, what stops afterwards (editing and the row counter; viewing and export remain), the JPY 3000 one-time unlock and that there is no automatic charge; 「7日間の無料体験をはじめる」 starts the JPY 0 trial IAP and × closes it. Restore is on the same sheet. One reminder appears in the last 24 hours and one notice when the trial ends.

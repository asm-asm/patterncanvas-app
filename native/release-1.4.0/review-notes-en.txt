Version 1.4.0 adds one offline feature: Settings (設定・保存) > 新しくつくる > 「本の編み図を撮って段数を数える」. The user photographs a knitting chart from a book or a printout (「カメラで撮る」 opens the camera; 「写真から選ぶ」 opens the photo picker), drags four handles onto the corners of the printed grid, and the app flattens the photo (perspective correction) and reads the row lines on the device. The user checks and can correct the row count, can join the two pages of a spread (or a second block above in the same photo), and then counts rows on the photo itself: the current row is highlighted with a band, with the same 「この段を完了」/「戻る」 counter, row notes and direction setting as for drawn charts. Nothing is recognised or redrawn (no OCR, no AI) and nothing is uploaded; the flattened image is stored on the device as part of the project and included in the user's own JSON export. The camera and photo-library purpose strings were updated to describe this use. Creating such a project and counting rows follow the same trial/lock rules as the other editing features. To try it, any photo of a printed grid (for example a sheet of graph paper) works.

1.3.2 changed only the first run: the sample chart is a three-colour Fair Isle OXO band (original design), and the app opens straight into it and the guide (see the business-model paragraph below).

1.3.1 added one offline editing option: below the yarn palette, 「この色を変更」 opens the standard iOS colour picker for the selected yarn; every cell painted with that yarn takes the new colour (cells keep their yarn, so it is undoable with 「元に戻す」). The same works by tapping a yarn swatch in Settings (設定・保存) > 毛糸の色.

1.3.0 added one offline feature: Settings (設定・保存) > 作品を保存・読み込み > 「編み図をPDFで書き出す（印刷用）」. The chart pages are rendered on the device and handed to the standard share sheet (Print, Save to Files, etc.). Nothing is uploaded and no network is used. It works while the chart is locked and after the trial, because it only reads the project.

Features from earlier versions, all fully on-device with no network, account or server:
- 1.2.2: 「文字から編み図をつくる」 has a 書体 (typeface) menu with six bundled fonts (Misaki, PixelMplus, Zen Maru Gothic, Dela Gothic One, Yusei Magic, Pacifico), all licensed for commercial use and redistribution (SIL OFL 1.1, M+ FONT LICENSE, Misaki licence); licences and credits are linked from the in-app help page (使い方).
- 1.2.1: 「丸ヨークの編み図をつくる」 calculates a top-down yoke from gauge and size. The standard StoreKit review request (AppStore.requestReview) is called at most once, after 20 completed rows, never at launch or around purchase sheets.
- 1.2.0: 「写真から2〜3色の編み図をつくる」 converts a user-chosen photo into a 2- or 3-yarn chart locally; the photo never leaves the device.

Business model (unchanged since 1.1.0): free download + non-consumable 7-day trial (jp.amimononote.ios.trial7, JPY 0) + non-consumable permanent unlock (jp.amimononote.ios.lifetime, JPY 3000). No subscription, login or external payment. The app opens into the sample chart and a skippable three-step guide; the trial terms sheet is shown when the user first tries to edit or count (and from the top banner). It states the 7-day duration, what stops afterwards (editing and the row counter; viewing and export remain), the JPY 3000 one-time unlock and that there is no automatic charge; 「7日間の無料体験をはじめる」 starts the JPY 0 trial IAP and × closes it. Restore is on the same sheet. One reminder appears in the last 24 hours and one notice when the trial ends.
